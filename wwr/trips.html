Whitewater · Guided run

Los Dientes del Diablo

Take on a memorable stretch of whitewater with a guide leading the way. Get oriented to the river, practice your paddle commands, and enjoy the canyon scenery between rapids.

Best for: Rafters looking for lively water

Included: Raft, paddle, fitted life jacket, helmet, and guide

Whitewater · Guided run

Big Mallard

Follow your guide through a lively rapid and the quieter water that follows. This outing pairs teamwork and exciting paddle moments with time to take in the river corridor.

Best for: New and returning rafters

Included: Raft, paddle, fitted life jacket, helmet, splash jacket, and guide

Half day · Guided trip

Half Day Adventure

Make a day of it on the river, with a mix of moving water, calmer stretches, and scenic canyon views. Your guide helps the group find a comfortable rhythm from launch to takeout.

Best for: Guests wanting a longer river outing

Included: Raft, paddle, fitted life jacket, helmet, and guide
