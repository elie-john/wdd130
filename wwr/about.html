Riverstone Rafting

We turn untamed water into shared stories. Our guide-owned crew pairs local river knowledge with warm, practical hospitality so every guest can feel the thrill, the connection, and the confidence to paddle on.

Our story

History

Riverstone began in 2008 with two boats, a hand-painted sign, and a belief that the river belongs to everyone. What started as weekend trips for neighbors has grown into a close-knit team of certified guides, river stewards, and adventure hosts. We still choose the quieter put-ins, learn every bend, and celebrate every first splash.
