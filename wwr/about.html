White Water Rafting

We turn untamed water into shared stories. Our guide-owned crew pairs local river knowledge with warm, practical hospitality so every guest can feel the thrill, the connection, and the confidence to paddle on.

Our story

History

White Water Rafting began in 2008 with two boats, a hand-painted sign, and a belief that the river belongs to everyone. What started as weekend trips for neighbors has grown into a close-knit team of guides, river stewards, and adventure hosts.

Every trip begins with a safety briefing and a look at the river ahead. Our guides share local stories, point out wildlife, and help guests feel ready before the first paddle stroke. By the end of the day, strangers leave as friends with a story to tell.
